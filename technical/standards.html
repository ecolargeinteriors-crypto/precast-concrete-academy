Standards, applicability and evidence status.

This register prevents a standard, test method or structural claim from being applied to the wrong product. The applicable edition and Kenyan adoption must be confirmed against the contract, statutory requirements and current standards catalogue.

Citation is not certification

Naming a standard does not prove conformity. Certification requires the applicable inspection, sampling, testing, factory controls and authorization.

Edition control

Every controlled document should record its title, edition, source, purchase date, revision status, product scope and responsible reviewer.

Project precedence

The approved project specification, statutory requirements and engineer’s design govern the work. PCA guidance cannot override them.

KEBS product-certification pathway

  1. 01 — ScopeIdentify the exact product standard and declared use.
  2. 02 — ControlDocument incoming materials, in-process checks and finished-product inspection.
  3. 03 — TestUse KEBS or an appropriately accredited/designated laboratory for required tests.
  4. 04 — DeclarePublish only properties supported by current reports and valid authorization.
