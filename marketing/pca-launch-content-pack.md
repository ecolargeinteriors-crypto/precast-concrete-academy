# PCA 14-Day Launch Content Pack

Use the product or resource link in every post. Replace claims with verified customer evidence before using testimonials or performance figures.

## Facebook and LinkedIn posts

1. **Stop costing rejected blocks as if they were saleable.** If a batch consumes cement, labour, power and mould time but produces breakage, the accepted units must carry that cost. Download our free accepted-unit calculator and see what your factory is actually earning.

2. **Curing is not storage.** A closed room is not automatically a controlled curing system. Record start time, moisture protection, interruptions and release evidence. Read our free curing lesson.

3. **Why do blocks break?** First identify when the damage occurs: demoulding, transfer, curing, loading or use. Each stage points to different causes. Use the PCA QC Checklist to investigate before adding more cement blindly.

4. **Planning a precast factory?** Begin with products, accepted demand and bottlenecks—not a machinery catalogue. The Factory Setup & Operations Manual covers capacity, layouts, utilities, commissioning and working capital.

5. **Pumice can reduce unit weight, but cheap aggregate does not guarantee a cheap accepted unit.** Moisture, absorption, density, breakage and strength all need controlled trials. The Block & Hollow-Pot Manual shows the trial path.

6. **A low selling price can hide expensive leakage.** Freight, under-loaded trucks, site waiting, discounts, credit and rejected units all reduce pocket margin. The Costing, Pricing & Sales Manual connects the whole commercial chain.

7. **Five manuals. One factory-control pathway.** Engineering, production, mix design, factory operations and commercial control—built for people who need practical systems, not vague inspiration.

## Three 25-second reel scripts

### Reel 1 — Why blocks break
**Hook:** “Adding more cement may not solve your broken blocks.”  
**Sequence:** Show demoulding → transfer → curing → loading. Explain that the stage of failure narrows the cause.  
**CTA:** “Download the free PCA Concrete Block QC Checklist.”

### Reel 2 — Accepted-unit cost
**Hook:** “You moulded 100 blocks—but how many did you actually sell?”  
**Sequence:** Display 100 moulded, 4 rejected, 2 broken, 94 accepted. Divide total batch cost by 94, not 100.  
**CTA:** “Get the free PCA Factory Tools Excel pack.”

**Hook:** “Do not buy the machine before you map the factory.”
