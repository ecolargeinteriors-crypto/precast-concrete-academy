Two-week curriculum

From raw materials to accepted stock.

Week 01

Materials and production control

  1. 1. Precast production principles and product families
  2. 2. Cement, quarry dust, sand, aggregates, pumice, water and admixtures
  3. 3. Receiving inspection, grading and aggregate-moisture control
  4. 4. Weigh batching, gauge-box control and safe mixing
  5. 5. Controlled trials and consistency checks
  6. 6. Hollow-block and solid-block production
  7. 7. Slab hollow-pot production and engineering boundaries
  8. 8. Mould filling, vibration, compaction and demoulding
  9. 9. Green-product handling and curing
  10. 10. Dimensions, density, testing and batch records
Week 02

Products and factory operations

  1. 1. Cabro and paving-unit production
  2. 2. Precast beam production principles and mould preparation
  3. 3. Culvert and drainage-product production principles
  4. 4. Reinforcement preparation, cover and traceability
  5. 5. Dry-cast and wet-cast operating differences
  6. 6. Machine operation and mould maintenance
  7. 7. Factory workflow, stockpiles and production planning
  8. 8. PPE, machine, lifting and housekeeping safety
  9. 9. Accepted-unit costing and production records
  10. 10. Defect investigation, stock control and dispatch

Important boundaries

Training production safely and responsibly.

Beam, culvert and floor-system sessions cover manufacturing principles, mould preparation, reinforcement control, handling, quality records and the interface with approved drawings. The course does not qualify learners to perform structural design or replace professional engineering approval.
