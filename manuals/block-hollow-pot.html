Questions before purchase

Does the manual give one universal mix ratio? +

No. It teaches controlled trials and laboratory verification because materials and machines differ.

Does it structurally design beam-and-block floors? +

No. Hollow pots are components of an engineer-designed floor system; structural design remains the engineer’s responsibility.

Is pumice production covered? +

Yes. It includes moisture conditioning, density, absorption, breakage, thermal benefits and cost-per-accepted-unit trials.
