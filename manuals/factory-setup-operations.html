Questions before purchase

Does it recommend one machine capacity? +

No. Capacity must be verified against product mix, bottlenecks, curing, labour and supplier guarantees.

Are the layouts construction drawings? +

No. They are workflow concepts. Civil, structural, electrical and environmental designs require qualified local professionals.

Does it cover mobile production? +

Yes. It includes temporary site operations, stockpile control, portable curing and generator-power consistency.
