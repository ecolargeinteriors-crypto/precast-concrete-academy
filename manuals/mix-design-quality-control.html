Questions before purchase

Is this a recipe book? +

No. It provides a controlled design-and-verification method rather than an unsafe universal recipe.

Does it cover dry-cast products? +

Yes. Dry-cast blocks, paving units and similar immediately demoulded products are treated separately from wet-cast concrete.

Can small factories use it? +

Yes. The controls can be scaled to simple equipment, provided weighing, sampling and verification remain disciplined.
