Why Concrete Blocks Break - and How to Investigate the Cause

Breakage is usually a process signal. Separate green handling damage, poor compaction, aggregate grading, moisture variation and inadequate curing before changing cement content.

Important: This educational article does not replace product specifications, laboratory testing, engineering design or locally applicable requirements.

1. Identify when the damage occurs

Damage during demoulding points to a different cause from breakage during loading or at 28 days. Record the stage, batch and handling event.

2. Check moisture and compaction

A dry-cast mix can look similar while compacting differently. Verify aggregate moisture, batch water, machine settings and fresh compacted density.

3. Audit curing exposure

Confirm when curing began, how moisture was retained and whether stacks experienced sun, wind or interrupted water supply.

4. Test instead of guessing

Use dimensional checks, density, absorption and strength testing on traceable samples. Change one controlled trial variable at a time.
