Curing Concrete Products: The Lowest-Cost Quality Upgrade

Curing protects the moisture and temperature conditions needed for cement hydration. It is a controlled process, not simply storing products behind a closed door.

Important: This educational article does not replace product specifications, laboratory testing, engineering design or locally applicable requirements.

Start immediately

Protect green products as soon as the handling method allows. Avoid drying winds, direct sun and uncontrolled movement.

Maintain the planned environment

Use wet coverings, misting, enclosures or other validated methods suited to the product and factory.

Record interruptions

Log water loss, power failures, enclosure openings and unusual weather so later test results can be investigated.

Release with evidence

Age alone is not proof. Use the specified curing record, inspection and test evidence before dispatch.
